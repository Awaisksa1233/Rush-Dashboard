# RUSH Dashboard — Executive KPI Calculation Guide

A business-level summary of how Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are defined and calculated across the RUSH executive dashboard.

---

## 1. Revenue & Growth

### Net Revenue
- **What It Measures**: Total revenue generated from subscription billings, renewals, single-wash retail purchases, and add-ons during the selected time period.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Net Revenue} = \text{Total Payments Collected} - \text{Refunds \& Chargebacks}$$
- **Period Comparison ($\Delta\%$)**:
  $$\Delta\% = \left( \frac{\text{Current Period Revenue} - \text{Previous Period Revenue}}{\text{Previous Period Revenue}} \right) \times 100\%$$

---

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- **What It Measures**: The predictable, normalized monthly subscription revenue from all active members.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Closing MRR} = \sum (\text{Active Members in Plan} \times \text{Monthly Plan Price})$$

---

### MRR Waterfall (Net Movement)
- **What It Measures**: How monthly recurring revenue changed from the start of the period to the close.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Net MRR Movement} = \text{New MRR} + \text{Expansion MRR} + \text{Reactivation MRR} - \text{Churned MRR} - \text{Delinquent MRR}$$

---

## 2. Membership, Churn & Retention

### Active Memberships
- **What It Measures**: Total count of active, paying members currently entitled to wash services.
- **Breakdown**:
  - **Auto-Renewing**: Members with active automated renewal billing enabled.
  - **Cancelled (Valid until expiry)**: Members who requested cancellation but whose pre-paid billing cycle is still running.

---

### Net Member Growth
- **What It Measures**: The net addition or loss of subscribers over the selected period.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Net Member Growth} = (\text{New Members} + \text{Reactivated Members}) - \text{Total Churned Members}$$

---

### Churn Rate
- **What It Measures**: The percentage of members lost within the period relative to the active customer base.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Churn Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Lost Members}}{\text{Starting Member Base}} \right) \times 100\%$$
- **Types of Churn**:
  - **Voluntary Churn**: Members who explicitly chose to cancel their subscription.
  - **Involuntary Churn**: Members lost due to repeated payment failures and expired payment cards.

---

### Renewal Collection Rate
- **What It Measures**: Efficiency in successfully collecting scheduled recurring membership renewals.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Renewal Collection Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{First-Try Payments Collected} + \text{Recovered Payments}}{\text{Total Renewals Scheduled}} \right) \times 100\%$$

---

## 3. Customer Economics & Lifetime Value

### Average Revenue Per Member (ARPM / ARPU)
- **What It Measures**: The average monthly revenue generated by each active subscriber.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{ARPM} = \frac{\text{Total Monthly MRR}}{\text{Total Active Members}}$$

---

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- **What It Measures**: Total revenue expected from a customer across their entire membership tenure.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{LTV} = \text{ARPM} \times \text{Average Customer Lifespan (in Months)}$$
  *(Average subscriber lifespan at RUSH is approximately 5 to 6 months).*

---

### Cohort Retention Rate
- **What It Measures**: Tracks signup groups by month and measures the percentage still active over time.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Retention Rate (Month } n) = \left( \frac{\text{Members Still Active in Month } n}{\text{Original Signup Cohort Count}} \right) \times 100\%$$
- **Standard Benchmarks**:
  - **Month 1**: $\ge 92\%$ (Onboarding success)
  - **Month 3**: $\ge 80\%$ (Core habit established)
  - **Month 6**: $\ge 72\%$ (Long-term loyalty)

---

## 4. Wash Operations & Utilization

### Average Washes per Member per Month
- **What It Measures**: Average frequency at which subscribers utilize the wash tunnel each month.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Average Washes / Member} = \frac{\text{Total Member Washes in Month}}{\text{Active Member Count}}$$

---

### Revenue per Wash
- **What It Measures**: Average gross revenue yield captured per tunnel wash event.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Revenue per Wash} = \frac{\text{Total Net Revenue}}{\text{Total Washes Completed}}$$

---

### Utilization Segmentation
- **0 Washes (Sleepers)**: Active paying subscribers who did not wash this month (high churn risk).
- **1–2 Washes**: Casual regulars.
- **3–5 Washes**: Optimal subscriber usage and highest lifetime retention.
- **6+ Washes**: Power users and commercial vehicle fleets.

---

## 5. Sales Team Performance

### New Member Pitch Conversion Rate
- **What It Measures**: Effectiveness of express lane advisors in converting first-time, prospective drive-up drivers into recurring subscribers. This metric is **strictly restricted to New Members** to provide an authentic measure of pipeline acquisition.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{New Member Conversion Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{New Subscriptions Sold}}{\text{New Customer Pitches Given}} \right) \times 100\%$$

---

### Winbacks ($\ge 2$ Months / 60 Days Inactive)
- **What It Measures**: Re-enrollment of former subscribers whose prior membership expired or was cancelled $\ge 2$ months (60 days) ago.
- **Classification Rules**:
  - **New Member**: First-time subscriber joining RUSH.
  - **Quick Reactivation**: Former subscriber rejoining within $< 60$ days of lapse.
  - **Winback**: Former subscriber re-enrolling after $\ge 60$ days of inactivity.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Winback Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Winbacks Closed}}{\text{Lapsed Inactive Members Pitched}} \right) \times 100\%$$
- **Tracking & Strategic Value**: Winbacks are tracked separately from new conversions to keep acquisition metrics clean while driving lapsed member recovery.

---

### Quota Attainment
- **What It Measures**: Sales team progress against monthly subscription sales targets.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Quota Attainment (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Actual Sales}}{\text{Target Quota}} \right) \times 100\%$$

---

### Sales Advisor Leaderboard Rank (Model A)
- **What It Measures**: Performance ranking of lane sales advisors combining acquisition closing efficiency, subscription tier quality, and one-off upsell value.
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{Rank Score} = (0.30 \times \text{Norm}(CR_{\text{new}})) + (0.40 \times \text{Norm}(P_{\text{membership}})) + (0.30 \times \text{Norm}(P_{\text{onetime}}))$$
- **The 3 Pillars**:
  1. **New Member Conversion Rate ($CR_{\text{new}}$, 30% weight)**: % of new prospective vehicle pitches converted into paid first-time subscriptions.
  2. **Avg Membership Sales Price ($P_{\text{membership}}$, 40% weight)**: Average price of subscription deals closed (rewards selling Nano Ceramic 169 SAR vs Fresh 69 SAR).
  3. **Avg One-Time Sales Price ($P_{\text{onetime}}$, 30% weight)**: Average price of single-wash orders & interior details sold.
- **Normalization**: Each component is normalized on a $0 - 100$ scale across team min/max bounds:
  $$\text{Norm}(M) = \left( \frac{M - \min(M)}{\max(M) - \min(M)} \right) \times 100$$
- **Note on Winbacks**: Winbacks ($\ge 60$ days lapsed) are tracked and rewarded as a high-value volume KPI and column on the leaderboard, keeping $CR_{\text{new}}$ isolated to new acquisition.

## 6. Signup → Subscription Funnel

Endpoint: `GET /api/funnel/signup-subscription?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD&window=7|30|60|90` (dates in Riyadh time, UTC+3). UI: **Analytics Hub → Signup Funnel**.

### Cohort and conversion window
- **Cohort**: customers in `rushwash_customers` whose `createdAt` falls in `[from, to]`.
- **Window ($W$)**: each customer is followed for $W$ days from *their own* signup time.
- **Mature customers only**: a customer enters the rates only once $\text{signup} + W \le \text{now}$. Customers still inside their window are reported separately ("still in window") so recent cohorts do not look like drop-off.
- **Tracking floor**: `from` is clamped to **2026-07-23**, when membership records start in `rushwash_subscriptions`. Earlier cohorts would show ~0% conversion because of missing data, not customer behaviour.

### Steps (cumulative)
| # | Step | Customer reaches it when… |
|---|------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Signed up | account created in the cohort range |
| 2 | Came back | ≥ 1 wash in `rushwash_wash_histories` within $W$ days, **or** subscribed within $W$ |
| 3 | Repeat visitor | ≥ 2 washes within $W$ days, **or** subscribed within $W$ |
| 4 | Subscribed | first top-level subscription (`parentSubCode: null`) created within $W$ days |

Steps are cumulative because ~1 in 3 members subscribe at signup, before any wash is logged; a strict wash → repeat → subscribe order would undercount them. A wash logged up to 1 day before the account record still counts (same-visit tolerance).

$$\text{Step conversion}_n = \frac{\text{Customers at step } n}{\text{Customers at step } n-1} \qquad \text{Overall conversion} = \frac{\text{Subscribed}}{\text{Signed up}}$$

### Time to subscribe
Days from customer `createdAt` to first subscription `createdAt` (negative lags from back-dated records are floored at 0). Reported as median / P75 / P95 and buckets: signup day (< 1 day), 1–7, 8–30, 31+.

### MRR at stake
$$\text{MRR at stake} = \text{Repeat visitors who never subscribed} \times \overline{\text{first-membership price}}$$
An upper bound on recoverable monthly recurring revenue, not a forecast.

### Segments
- **Signup source**: `createdByShopId` set → branch signup; `createdById = "portal-signup"` → online portal; otherwise "source not recorded".
- **First visit type**: `planType` of the customer's first wash (exterior vs interior), or "no wash recorded".
